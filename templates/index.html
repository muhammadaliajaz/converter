FAQs

Everything you need to know about file conversions, marksheet OCR, privacy, and limits.

How do I convert Word to PDF online free?
Simply click on the "Word to PDF" card, upload your DOC or DOCX document from your device, and click Process Document. Your converted, publication-ready PDF is downloaded instantly.
Will my PDF tables and layout break when converted to Word?
No! Our layout-preservation algorithm detects table borders, cell alignments, nested rows, and fonts. It maps them directly into native Word tables so your converted DOCX looks identical to the original PDF without scattered text or misaligned lines.
How does the Marksheet to Excel OCR feature work?
Upload a photo or scan of any student marksheet, report card, or billing table. Our AI OCR engine automatically extracts columns, subject names, numbers, and grades into an interactive editable table. You can edit any cell directly on screen before downloading as a clean .xlsx spreadsheet.
Is this service really 100% free with no limits?
Yes, completely free forever. There are zero subscriptions, no credit cards required, and no hidden trial periods. You do not even need to create an account or provide an email.
Are my uploaded files and data safe and private?
Yes. All file transfers occur over end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption. Documents are processed in temporary memory and shredded immediately after download. We never read, store, or sell any of your content.
Can I access SmartFile on mobile devices?
SmartFile is fully responsive and optimized for iPhones, Android smartphones, tablets, and desktops. The converter opens smoothly in a lightweight mobile workspace without page reloads.
